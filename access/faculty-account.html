Get an HPC account

Sharanga accounts are given to BITS faculty members. If your guide does not have one yet, your guide fills in the account request form.

Steps

  1. The faculty member opens the account request form.
  2. They fill it in and submit it.
  3. The HPC team creates the account and replies with the username.
  4. Students can then be added to that account (next page).
Open the account request form

Form link: https://forms.gle/XudgYysqLG15gbgNA

Students cannot request an account for themselves. Access always goes through a faculty account.
