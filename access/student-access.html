Access · 2 of 3 · For students

Get access to your guide's account

If your guide already has a Sharanga account, email the HPC team with your guide in CC and attach your SSH public key. They add your key to your guide's account.

Before you write

  1. Ask your guide for their Sharanga username. No account yet? See Faculty account.
  2. Create your SSH key and find your .pub file. See Create your SSH public key.

Write to the HPC team

Email hpc@hyderabad.bits-pilani.ac.in (High Performance Computing) with your guide in CC. Attach your SSH public key (the .pub file) and ask them to add it to your guide's account.

Once you get access

Log in from the same laptop where you created the key. No password is needed.

ssh <guide's username>@hpc.bits-hyderabad.ac.in
