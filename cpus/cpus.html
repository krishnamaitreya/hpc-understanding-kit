CPU nodes on Sharanga

Each row is a partition. You write its name in your job script. Pick by how many cores your job needs: the two big partitions only accept jobs of 128 cores or more.

PartitionCores per jobNodesCores / RAM per nodeMax job timeYou can run at once
compute4 to 642264 / 256 GB4 days7 jobs, 1,024 cores in total
big_compute128 or more19192 / 385 GB5 days8 jobs
big_compute_amd_9655128 or more5192 / 385 GB5 days2 jobs

Which one should I pick?

  • Fewer than 128 cores (most jobs): compute.
  • 128 cores or more (large parallel jobs): big_compute.
  • 128 cores or more, newest CPUs: big_compute_amd_9655, but only 2 jobs at a time and it is often full.
Always name the partition. A job without one goes to big_compute. If it asks for fewer than 128 cores, it waits forever with the reason QOSMinCpuNotSatisfied.
Limits are per account, shared by everyone using the same guide's account. The partitions big_compute_physics_hyd, big_compute_physics_hyd_comsol and big_compute_suryoday belong to specific groups. Don't use them.

Checked on the cluster on 30 Sep 2026. Limits can change.
