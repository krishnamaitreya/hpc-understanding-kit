Check which CPUs are free

Run these on the HPC after you log in.

1 · sinfo: free cores per partition

sinfo -p compute,big_compute,big_compute_amd_9655 -o "%P %l %D %C"
HPC · real output, 30 Sep 2026
PARTITION TIMELIMIT NODES CPUS(A/I/O/T)
compute infinite 22 924/484/0/1408
big_compute* 5-00:00:00 19 1920/1536/192/3648
big_compute_amd_9655 5-00:00:00 5 960/0/0/960

CPUS(A/I/O/T) means Allocated / Idle / Other (down) / Total. The second number is the free cores.

compute shows TIMELIMIT infinite, but your jobs there are still capped at 4 days.

2 · cpufree: free cores and idle nodes

One-time setup. Paste this once on the HPC.

cat >> ~/.bashrc <<'EOF'
cpufree(){ for p in compute big_compute big_compute_amd_9655; do c=$(sinfo -h -p $p -o "%C"); i=$(sinfo -h -p $p -t idle -o "%D"); echo "$c" | awk -F/ -v p=$p -v i=${i:-0} '{printf "%-22s free %5d of %5d cores   %2d whole nodes idle\n", p, $2, $4, i}'; done; }
EOF
source ~/.bashrc

Then, any time:

cpufree
HPC · real output, 30 Sep 2026
[<username>@hpc01 ~]$ cpufree
compute                free   484 of  1408 cores    0 whole nodes idle
big_compute            free  1536 of  3648 cores    7 whole nodes idle
big_compute_amd_9655   free     0 of   960 cores    0 whole nodes idle

Per node

To see each node in a partition, with free cores and free memory (MB):

sinfo -N -p big_compute -o "%N %t %C %e"
