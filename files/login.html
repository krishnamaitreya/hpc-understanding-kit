Log in

Open a terminal on your laptop (cmd on Windows, Terminal on macOS and Linux) and run one command. <username> is your guide's Sharanga username.

ssh <username>@hpc.bits-hyderabad.ac.in

The first time only

SSH asks whether you trust the server. Type yes and press Enter.

your laptop · first login
you@laptop:~$ ssh <username>@hpc.bits-hyderabad.ac.in
The authenticity of host 'hpc.bits-hyderabad.ac.in (125.22.54.219)' can't be established.
ED25519 key fingerprint is SHA256:jBqh4Eg7Un/yLtgt+8jLN6MF1C/61NBn1I6cbyPXxh0.
Are you sure you want to continue connecting (yes/no/[fingerprint])? yes

The fingerprint should match SHA256:jBqh4Eg7Un/yLtgt+8jLN6MF1C/61NBn1I6cbyPXxh0. If it doesn't, stop and tell the HPC team.

Connection to hpc.bits-hyderabad.ac.in closed.

You are now on the login node, hpc01. Use it to edit files, upload data and submit jobs. Do not run training here. Training runs on GPU nodes through Slurm (a later topic).

Type exit to log out.

Sharing your guide's account?

Other students may use the same account. Keep your work in your own folder:

mkdir -p /scratch/$USER/<yourname>
Asked for a password? Your key has not been added yet, or you are on a different laptop from the one where you created the key.
