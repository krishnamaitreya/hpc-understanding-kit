The CSIS node

CSIS has its own GPU node, gpunode8, with 6 NVIDIA RTX Pro 6000 GPUs (96 GB each). You reach it through one partition name, if your account is enabled for it.

Nodegpunode8
GPUs6 × RTX Pro 6000 Blackwell, 96 GB each
CPU / RAM64 cores, 1 TB
Partitiongpu_rtx_pro_6000_6_csis_hyd
Max job time2 days
You can use at once3 GPUs, 24 CPU cores, 300 GB RAM
Your account must be enabled for this node. Only accounts the HPC team has enabled can use it. If sbatch says Invalid qos specification, your guide's account is not enabled. Ask your guide to request it from hpc@hyderabad.bits-pilani.ac.in.

Is a GPU free on it?

gpufree | grep csis

Who is using it right now:

squeue -p gpu_rtx_pro_6000_6_csis_hyd

Use it in a job

Put this line in your job script. The Submit a job topic shows the full script.

#SBATCH --partition=gpu_rtx_pro_6000_6_csis_hyd
