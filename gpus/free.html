Check which GPUs are free

Run these on the HPC after you log in. sinfo shows how busy each partition is. gpufree counts the free GPUs.

1 · sinfo: how busy is each partition?

A - after the state (e.g. mix-) means Slurm is holding that node for a waiting job.

2 · gpufree: how many GPUs are free?

One-time setup. Paste this once on the HPC. It adds a gpufree command to your account.

cat >> ~/.bashrc <<'EOF'
gpufree(){ sinfo -h -N -o "%N %P %t" | grep ^gpunode | while read n p s; do t=$(scontrol show node $n); tot=$(grep -oP 'CfgTRES=\S*?gres/gpu=\K[0-9]+' <<<"$t"); use=$(grep -oP 'AllocTRES=\S*?gres/gpu=\K[0-9]+' <<<"$t"); printf "%-9s %-28s %-6s free %s of %s\n" $n $p $s $((tot-${use:-0})) $tot; done; }
EOF
source ~/.bashrc

Then, any time:

gpufree

A free GPU is not a promise. Someone may take it before your job starts. If none is free, submit anyway and your job waits in the queue. gpunode1 is reserved for Schrödinger software users.
