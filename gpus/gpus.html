Limits are per account. If several students share one guide's account, they share these limits too.

Which one should I pick?

  • Long training (more than 1 day): A100 (up to 5 days) or H100 (up to 3 days).
  • Short runs, tests, evaluation: H200 (up to 1 day).
  • CSIS students: the CSIS partition (up to 2 days, 3 GPUs), if your guide's account is enabled for it. See The CSIS node.
  • Small models: V100, which is often less busy.

Checked on the cluster on 30 Sep 2026. Limits can change. Check the current time limit with sinfo -p <partition> (TIMELIMIT column).
