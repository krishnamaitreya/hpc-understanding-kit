Privacy

This site does not collect personal information. It has no accounts, sign-up forms, cookies, analytics or advertising.

What stays in your browser

Pages with a Windows, macOS and Linux choice remember the system you last picked. This is saved in your browser's local storage, stays on your device, and is never sent anywhere. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes it.

The Copy buttons write text to your clipboard on your device. The site never reads your clipboard.

The job script builder runs entirely in your browser. What you type into it is not saved or sent anywhere.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any website it hosts, GitHub receives technical information when you visit, such as your IP address. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

The site loads no fonts, scripts or trackers from other services.

Links to other sites

Some pages link to other sites, such as the official Sharanga documentation and the faculty account form on Google Forms. When you follow those links, that site's own privacy policy applies.
