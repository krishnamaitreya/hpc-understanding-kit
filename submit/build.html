Build your job script

Choose GPU or CPU, fill in the form, and copy the script. It checks your choices against Sharanga's limits as you type.

Your script

Save it as job.sh in your project folder on the HPC.


  


  

    
Two lines you must never skip

    
    
      
  • --time: without it, Sharanga stops your job after 30 minutes.
    • 
      
  • --mem: without it, a CPU job takes the whole node's memory and blocks everyone else on that node.
    • 
    

  


  

    
What each line means

    

      
LineMeaning

      
        
#!/bin/bashRun the script with bash. Always the first line.

        
--job-nameA name for your job. Shown in squeue and used in log file names.

        
--partitionWhich group of nodes to run on (see Find free GPUs / CPU nodes).

        
--nodes=1, --ntasks=1One node, one program. Keep these as they are.

        
--cpus-per-taskCPU cores. For training, match your dataloader workers.

        
--gres=gpu:1Number of GPUs. GPU jobs only.

        
--memMemory (RAM) for the job.

        
--timeMaximum run time as days-hours:minutes:seconds. The job is stopped when it runs out.

        
--outputFile that collects everything your program prints. %x = job name, %j = job ID.

        
--errorFile that collects errors and warnings.

        
source ~/.bashrc
conda activate		Load conda and switch to your environment.

        
cdGo to your project folder.

        
last lineThe command that does the work.
