Check your jobs

squeue --me lists every job of your account that is running or waiting. A job disappears from the list when it ends.

squeue --me
HPC · example
[<username>@hpc01 ~]$ squeue --me
  JOBID PARTITION     NAME     USER ST       TIME  NODES NODELIST(REASON)
 371755 gpu_a100_   my_job <username>  R   15:19:01      1 gpunode4
 372920 gpu_h100_   my_job <username> PD       0:00      1 (Priority)
ColumnMeaning
JOBIDThe number sbatch gave you
STState: R = running, PD = pending (waiting), CG = finishing
TIMEHow long it has been running
NODELIST(REASON)Running: the node it is on. Waiting: why it is waiting (see next page)

Other useful commands

CommandWhat it does
squeue --me --startSlurm's estimate of when a waiting job will start
tail -f my_job_371755.outWatch the job's output live (Ctrl+C stops watching)
scancel 371755Cancel one job
scancel --meCancel all jobs of your account. Careful: on a shared account this includes other students' jobs.
scontrol show job 371755Every detail of a job: resources, node, times, reason
On a shared guide's account, squeue --me also shows the other students' jobs. Check the job name and ID before you cancel anything.
